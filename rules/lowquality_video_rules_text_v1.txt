LowqualityVideo Rules v3

Project scope
Review one e-commerce short video using video/product evidence fields plus is_AIGC. The workflow covers only these 15 issue types:
potential_pirated, irrelevant_promotion, pirated_content, inconsistent_product_promotion, non_native, still_frame, out_of_app_transactions, misleading_functionality_and_effect, dangerous_behavior, disgusting_and_terrifying, description_not_detailed, only_marketing_sales_pitches, pornography_perception, no_physical_product_display, unrealistic_or_continuity_error.

Do not cover the complete SOP. Do not output labels outside this list. Use none only when no in-scope issue is detected.

Cross-project rule adaptations
AIGC-IPP -> inconsistent_product_promotion: require promotion first, compare against all provided product images, compare packaging and content separately, use product_category only as exemption context, ignore OCR text / brand text / lighting / render effects / pure color differences alone, and do not force mismatch when the physical product is not shown.
AIGC-Misleading -> misleading_functionality_and_effect / unrealistic_or_continuity_error: keep hallucination, function exaggeration, false claim, and size/scale rules; check pattern identity drift, hardware layout drift, food or content identity drift, supported-function effect limits, storage-capacity exaggeration, toy/model scale effects, and product-reference food quantity or texture conflicts.
DND -> description_not_detailed: use 5 detail dimensions: function or efficacy, features or advantages, usage or scenario, parameters or specifications, and materials or ingredients. Subjective praise and hard-sell text do not count, and one sentence should not be double-counted across dimensions.
Only-Market-Pitch -> only_marketing_sales_pitches: hit only when ASR/OCR is hard-sell marketing such as price, discount, sale, hurry, limited stock, add to cart, checkout, free shipping, bundle, or gift, with no substantive product information.
video-product-lowquality -> workflow and evidence safeguards: keep GitHub raw rules, Rules Brain, Evidence Gate, single LLM, Output Validator, product-image-first visual manifest, product-local image indices, and frame_list adjacent-frame semantics.

Frame sampling contract
frame_list contains up to 8 video frames uniformly sampled across the full video duration in chronological order. Adjacent video frames mean neighboring sampled frames in frame_list, not frames one second apart. Do not claim exact elapsed time between frames unless explicit timestamps are provided.

is_AIGC
is_AIGC is an attribute, not a violation. is_AIGC only increases attention for unrealistic_or_continuity_error. It does not increase attention for misleading_functionality_and_effect, no_physical_product_display, still_frame, only_marketing_sales_pitches, pornography_perception, potential_pirated, pirated_content, or any other issue type. is_AIGC must not trigger any issue by itself. Every hit still requires concrete frame, ASR, OCR, or product image evidence.

Evidence scope
Use only video frames, ASR, OCR, and product evidence. Do not use external Pearl fields, Similar Video tab, creator profile, account ownership, penalty records, creator history, or feedback fields. Pirated and potential pirated must be judged only from current workflow inputs: video frames, ASR, OCR, and product evidence.

Decision values
problematic: one or more in-scope issue types have high-confidence evidence.
manual_review: evidence is suspicious but incomplete, required evidence is missing, or schema parsing fails.
clean: no in-scope issue is supported by available evidence.

Issue rules

1. potential_pirated
Potential pirated means suspicious spliced, remixed, or repurposed content.
Hit when video frames show frequent clip switching with different people, hands, scenery, lighting, resolution, aspect ratio, or production styles that strongly suggest multi-source splicing; sandwich-style or AB-style composition; or domestic-seller / other-creator footage repurposed for a European e-commerce promotion.
Required evidence: strong internal evidence from video frames, ASR, or OCR, with specific explanation of why ordinary montage is unlikely. No external Pearl fields are available or required.
Do not hit for ordinary editing, normal montage, product b-roll, weak style changes, or likely authorized brand/seller material without strong internal evidence. If suspicious but not conclusive, use manual_review and explain the evidence gap.

2. irrelevant_promotion
Irrelevant promotion means the video content is unrelated to the bound e-commerce product.
Hit when the main video is entertainment, storytelling, social content, accident footage, or unrelated scenes, and the bound product appears only briefly, flashes by, cannot be identified, or is not meaningfully promoted.
Do not hit when the video clearly demonstrates, explains, or uses the bound product in a substantive way, even if it also contains entertainment elements.

3. pirated_content
Pirated content means confirmed non-original or copied creator content.
Hit only with strong evidence such as visible third-party creator watermark, username, platform mark, repost mark, copied-source indicator, clear ASR/OCR saying the clip was copied/reposted/taken from another creator, or a duet-like layout where the creator adds no meaningful new product promotion and only carries another creator's product promotion.
Do not use feedback fields to judge pirated_content. Do not hit for ordinary montage, public-resource footage, or likely authorized brand/seller material without strong internal evidence. If suspicious but not conclusive, use manual_review.

4. inconsistent_product_promotion
Inconsistent product promotion means the video promotes one e-commerce product while the bound product reference images show a materially different product.
Hit when video ASR/OCR or frames promote an EC product, but the displayed product differs from the bound product in type, shape, structure, packaging, material, style, size, variant, package format, quantity, food texture, or core design.
Product images are required for a strong hit. Compare against all provided product images before deciding mismatch. Check packaging and content separately: packaging match alone does not clear content mismatch, and content match alone does not clear packaging mismatch.
Exempt blind boxes, accessories, jade, and collectible cards when displayed and bound products belong to the same L3 category. Exempt apparel tops and bottoms when the displayed product is a SKU variant with only pattern or color differences. product_category is only exemption context, not visual proof of consistency. Do not hit from OCR text, brand text, lighting, render effects, small accessories, decoration, or pure color differences alone.

5. non_native
Non-native means Chinese or incomprehensible Chinglish style in an international market.
Hit when the creator uses long Chinese spoken expression, Chinese characters in text/stickers/background, or spoken English that is highly unusual or too grammatically broken to understand.
Do not hit for slight accent when intelligible, or isolated non-local background speech that is not the creator's promotion. This workflow does not cover the broader Other Languages personalized category.

6. still_frame
Still frame means no product explanation and no real video footage.
Hit when there is no verbal or written product explanation and the product is displayed through static images, live photos, screenshots, cutouts, or pasted product images.
Do not hit when the video has meaningful real demonstration, even if some static slides are present. Do not hit from missing ASR/OCR alone if frames show clear product use.

7. out_of_app_transactions
Out-of-app transactions require both an external transaction route and purchase direction.
Hit when the content includes PayPal, Venmo, Cash App, WhatsApp, Telegram, phone number, external website, offline store, or another payment app, and directs viewers to purchase, pay, contact, or complete the transaction through that route.
Evidence can come from ASR, OCR, or visual signage in video frames.
Do not hit when external contact has unclear commercial intent, when the host clearly directs users to order inside TTS and the external mention is incidental, or when the external route is only for users who cannot access the TTS cart unless evasion is explicit.

8. misleading_functionality_and_effect
Misleading functionality and effect means the video materially exaggerates product function, effect, size, quality, capacity, safety, speed, or result.
Hit when exaggerated filming, unrealistic information, impossible before/after effects, rapid or guaranteed outcomes, fake expert/medical endorsement, unrealistic cleaning/repair/beauty/health/weight/growth result, impossible performance, unsupported quantity/weight/texture/package-count impression, or impossible capacity/organizing effect creates unreliable product expectations.
Supported functions do not automatically make effect strength realistic. Mist, spray, cooling, light, motion, cleaning, cooking, blending, or storage demonstrations must still stay within same-category realistic capability.
Do not hit for normal positive marketing praise without concrete misleading function or effect. Do not hit kitchen or cooking videos for normal jump cuts, steam, browning, finished-food beauty shots, or omitted waiting time unless there is an impossible process, false time/effect claim, or input/output inconsistency. Do not treat OCR numbers, platform UI counts, screen garbage, or ambiguous digits as false claims unless they clearly bind to product sales, rating, efficacy, certification, ranking, weight, or quantity. Do not hit just because is_AIGC is true.

9. dangerous_behavior
Dangerous behavior means unsafe or violent behavior that endangers life safety.
Hit when content shows or encourages dangerous imitation, violent acts, unsafe product operation, or product demonstrations that create the illusion of fire, heavy smoke, explosion, weapon-like harm, or other life-safety risk.
Do not hit for ordinary safe product use or decorative danger cues without realistic safety risk or unsafe promotional instruction.

10. disgusting_and_terrifying
Disgusting and terrifying means the content creates clear viewer discomfort.
Hit for ear cleaning, close-up insects, terrifying atmosphere, dirty drains, sewers, dirty or messy places, bodily material, sudden screams, harsh terrifying sounds, or physically uncomfortable audio/visual experience.
Do not hit for ordinary cleaning-product scenarios where a mildly dirty environment is acceptable, or minor background noise that does not block the main speaker.

11. description_not_detailed
Description not detailed means the video promotes a product but does not give enough product explanation.
Most products can be acceptable if clearly and fully displayed. Electric products, digital products, skincare products, and health supplements must be both displayed and explained. Use the 5 detail dimensions: function or efficacy, features or advantages, usage or scenario, parameters or specifications, and materials or ingredients.
Subjective praise such as amazing, good, beautiful, I love this, and hard-sell text do not count as product detail. Do not double-count one sentence across multiple dimensions. If ASR appears polluted or unrelated to the displayed product, prefer manual_review instead of a confident hit.
Do not hit for makeup get-ready-with-me videos, card unboxing/break videos, or party board game intro videos when the SOP exemption conditions are met. Do not confuse this with only_marketing_sales_pitches.

12. only_marketing_sales_pitches
Only marketing sales pitches means the speech or text is only hard-sell marketing.
Hit when ASR/OCR only emphasizes price, discount, gifts, promotion ending, stock pressure, urgency, checkout, cart, purchase guidance, free shipping, bundle, last chance, do not miss, limited stock, or similar hard-sell marketing, and has no substantive product function, specification, usage effect, usage method, material, compatibility, capacity, ingredient, or scenario explanation. This applies to all product categories.
Do not hit when there is weak but real product function, usage, specification, material, ingredient, compatibility, capacity, or scenario information. Do not collapse description_not_detailed into this label. Do not hit from missing ASR/OCR alone.

13. pornography_perception
Pornography perception means sexualized or vulgar presentation.
Hit when content contains unnecessary exposure or sexually suggestive action such as chest shaking, bending over to expose cleavage, prominent genital outlines, or intentionally sexualized body display; or the product itself creates high skin exposure and likely sensitive-body exposure.
Do not hit for ordinary introduction, display, or description of intimate products without private-part nudity and without sexually suggestive conduct. Regular bikini use in an appropriate beach context is not enough by itself.

14. no_physical_product_display
No physical product display means the content discusses the product but does not show the physical product.
Hit when there is verbal or written product explanation, but the product is shown only through stickers, cutouts, photos, screenshots, live images, webpage imagery, no visible product, or only a partial view that cannot confirm the bound product.
If the bound product includes package or brand but the video only shows the product result or usage outcome without package or brand, this can also support no physical product display.
Do not hit if the physical product is clearly shown and recognizable. If there is no product explanation, consider still_frame or irrelevant_promotion instead depending on evidence.

15. unrealistic_or_continuity_error
Unrealistic or continuity error means visual physics, identity, or continuity breaks that affect product understanding.
Hit when video frames show objects morphing, clipping, floating, moving on their own, passing through surfaces, changing geometry, product identity drift, structure drift, pattern drift, content identity drift, impossible scale or pose change, object materialization, garbled/morphing product text or logo, fake AIGC speech/interview, impossible before-after effects, unrealistic product size/style/effect, AI avatar explaining the product without product appearance, or storage/drawer compartment contents changing between adjacent sampled frames without visible placement.
Also check product surface pattern identity drift, hardware layout drift, food or content identity drift, small toy/model full-size environmental effects, wrong-location mist/smoke/light/liquid emission, and same-compartment continuity for transparent storage products.
When is_AIGC is true, pay extra attention to this issue type only. Still require concrete frame evidence.
Do not hit because is_AIGC is true by itself. Do not over-focus on minor artifacts that do not affect product understanding. Do not hit normal cooking color change, steaming, browning, shrinkage, folding, perspective, crop, occlusion, motion blur, low resolution, supported SKU switch, or ordinary lens distortion by itself. Do not claim exact elapsed time between frames without timestamps.

Data availability rules
If frame_list is empty, do not make video-frame visual claims.
If images is empty or invalid, do not make product-image comparison claims.
If ASR and OCR are empty, do not make spoken or written text claims.
Use only video frames, ASR, OCR, and product evidence for analysis in this workflow.
If evidence is suspicious but incomplete, prefer manual_review over a direct hit.

Output requirements
Return raw JSON only.
Use Chinese summary and evidence explanations.
Use only the 15 in-scope issue types plus none.
detected_issue_types is multi-select.
primary_issue_type must be one of detected_issue_types when overall_decision is problematic.
primary_issue_type must be none when overall_decision is clean.
Every hit or uncertain result must include concrete evidence.
is_AIGC must be returned as true, false, or unknown.
