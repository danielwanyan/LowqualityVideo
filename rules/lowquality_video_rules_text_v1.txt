LowqualityVideo Rules v6

This workflow is video-first. The final model judges only video low-quality signals.

Allowed model evidence: frame_list, ASR, OCR, images, country.
Display-only fields: url, product_id, seller_id_str, comments, product_review_summary.
Do not use display-only fields as judgment evidence.

Allowed final decisions: problematic, clean.
Forbidden final decision: manual_review.
Forbidden output: tagsProduct.

Frame sampling contract
frame_list contains up to 8 video frames uniformly sampled across the full video duration in chronological order.
Adjacent video frames mean neighboring sampled frames in frame_list, not frames one second apart.
Do not claim exact elapsed time between frames unless explicit timestamps are provided.

Human review output
The final JSON must include human_review_labels with ratings, tagsContent, and tagsAttribute. human_review_labels is multi-select; select all applicable labels, not only primary_issue_type.
ratings must be [video] when tagsContent is non-empty.
ratings must be [ok] when tagsContent is empty.
Do not output tagsProduct.
Only output 视频不可见 in tagsAttribute when video frames are missing or unusable.

Canonical business label priority
There are 15 business labels in total. The first 13 are low-quality issue labels ordered from highest to lowest priority:
1. suspected_pirated_or_reused_content
2. misleading_functionality_or_effect
3. unrealistic_or_continuity_error
4. pure_marketing_pitch
5. disgusting_or_terrifying_visual
6. sexual_or_vulgar_hook
7. description_not_detailed
8. out_of_app_transaction
9. pirated_content
10. still_frame
11. no_physical_product_display
12. irrelevant_product_promotion
13. video_product_mismatch
14. 无问题
15. 视频不可见
Return every supported issue in detected_issue_types in this order. primary_issue_type must be the first detected issue, or none when no issue passes.
无问题 is represented by ratings=[ok] and empty tagsContent. 视频不可见 is represented by tagsAttribute=[视频不可见]. Neither belongs in detected_issue_types.

Optional AIGC input contract
is_aigc is optional and accepts only numeric 0 or 1. Missing is not a third value and must not be rewritten as 0 or unknown.
is_aigc=1 is required but not sufficient for unrealistic_or_continuity_error; concrete visual abnormality evidence is also required.
is_aigc=0, missing, or invalid blocks unrealistic_or_continuity_error.
Never use is_aigc as evidence for suspected_pirated_or_reused_content, pirated_content, MFE, or any other issue.

Rule V1: video_product_mismatch / 挂车商品与讲解商品不一致
Hit when video frames or ASR/OCR clearly promote or explain a main product that conflicts with product images in core category, function, form, visible quantity, key specification, size, dimension, or promised use. Same product style does not clear a material size or dimension contradiction.
Do not hit for color-only differences, minor style/material/texture differences, lighting, angle, packaging language, ordinary brand-text differences, functional words printed on a product such as MUSIC SOUND, or a brief prop/background item. Do not use brand text, OCR text, product-page wording, or package language alone as IPP evidence unless the video is clearly promoting a different physical product.
Do not hit merely because the video shows more units than one product image when the sold set can include those units.

Rule V2: misleading_functionality_or_effect / 功能效果虚假夸大
Hit when frame_list, ASR, or OCR makes a concrete misleading claim about efficacy, speed, durability, medical or health effect, quantity, time, before/after result, safety, or physical capability. The claim must be supported by frame_list, ASR, or OCR from the video itself; product images are auxiliary only.
Do not hit from product images alone. Do not use product detail page claims or product image marketing copy by themselves to decide that the video is exaggerated. 商品详情页或商品图文案不能单独触发视频夸大。
Do not hit for ordinary advertising tone, subjective praise, metaphor, fashion or fit claims without concrete false promise, expected cleaning/shaving/styling/cooking outcomes, common category claims, or minor numerical ambiguity that can be ordinary speech error.
A concrete claim is not automatically misleading. It must be materially stronger than the bound product, contradicted by concrete product evidence, or shown through a physically impossible demonstration.
If the video claim is consistent with the bound product and is not materially more exaggerated, choose clean even when the wording is strong.

Rule V3: suspected_pirated_or_reused_content / 疑似盗版 / 疑似盗剪
Hit when the video strongly suggests reused or pirated content, such as Chinese domestic studio recording cues, Chinese UI/environment not fitting the target market, blurry reposted footage, watermark-hiding layout, or obvious unrelated creator/content reuse.
Do not hit for replying to a user comment, showing another creator briefly and then self-verifying, proof-style self-comparison, product validation, debunking content, same creator appearing in different scenes, generic blurry footage, simple edits, or style shifts without a concrete copied/reused-content signal.
is_aigc is not piracy evidence and must never support this label.

Rule V4: pure_marketing_pitch / 仅营销叫卖
Hit only when nearly all ASR/OCR is price, urgency, stock, discount, order guidance, or sales pressure, with almost no product function, material, size, use scenario, operation method, or realistic product information.
Weak but real product information should usually prevent this hit. Do not hit when the video provides substantive product features, material, size, usage, softness/comfort, compatibility, operation, or scenario information, even if it also contains buying guidance. Do not hit from price, discount, urgency, or purchase CTA alone when visual demonstration provides meaningful product information.
Visible wearing, trying on, operating, applying, assembling, or other real product use is substantive product content and blocks this label unless it is only a token moment and marketing pressure still dominates nearly the whole video.

Rule V5: no_physical_product_display / 无实物展示（围绕商品讲解）
Hit when the video talks about or promotes the product but does not show the physical product or a substantive product-use scene.
AI-generated style does not equal no physical product display.
Do not hit if the sampled frames clearly show the bound product or a substantive use scene.
A visible package, an identifiable product being worn, or a real application, operation, installation, or use action counts as physical product display.

Rule V6: irrelevant_product_promotion / 内容与商品无关推广
Hit when most of the video body is unrelated to the bound product, and the product appears only briefly, at the end, or not substantively.
Do not hit merely because the opening has setup or a short unrelated segment.
Do not hit if a later segment provides real product demonstration or explanation.
Do not hit when the product has substantive screen presence, including approximately one third of the sampled video, unless the remaining unrelated content clearly dominates the promotional message.

Rule V7: unrealistic_or_continuity_error / 穿帮 / 不符现实
is_aigc=1 is required before this issue can be considered. is_aigc=0, missing, or invalid blocks the issue.
is_aigc=1 only enables review and never creates a hit by itself.
Hit only when strong visual evidence affects consumer understanding of the product and cannot reasonably be explained by sampling interval, normal editing, camera angle, or missing intermediate action. Strong examples include directly taking an item from a sealed or unopened package without opening it, a connector, plug, hook, screw, support bar, or mounting structure changing impossibly, or a hand/object contact that cannot physically perform the shown action.
Do not hit for ordinary action jumps between sampled frames, missing intermediate actions alone, slight hand artifacts, lighting or angle changes, motion blur, or process-video cuts that do not affect product understanding.
If uncertain, choose clean.

Rule V8: sexual_or_vulgar_hook / 擦边低俗
Hit when the video uses a clear sexualized hook, vulgar, adult, dating, or sexual reward hook for promotion, including phrases such as spend the night with me.
Do not hit ordinary try-on, gym, swimwear, shapewear, or body-fit display without clear vulgar/adult framing.

Rule V9: disgusting_or_terrifying_visual / 恶心恐怖
Hit when the visual subject creates strong discomfort, dense-phobia-like reaction, injury, dirt, bodily, or horror-style effect.
Do not hit for mildly unattractive visuals or normal product demonstration.

Rule V10: pirated_content / 盗版内容
Hit when the video uses clearly pirated copyrighted video content as the main promotional body.
Do not hit merely for reference clips, reply/comment context, or same-creator multi-scene editing when product demonstration remains original.

Rule V11: description_not_detailed / 内容介绍不详细（低质）
Hit when the video fails to provide meaningful product information for categories where function or usage explanation is required, such as supplements, health products, electronics, tools, or appliances.
Do not hit for apparel, simple goods, or visual products when the product is clearly shown.
Do not hit when visual demonstration itself provides enough product information. For simple apparel, accessories, plush toys, lamps, decor, and similar visual goods, clear product display is usually sufficient unless the category needs function or safety explanation.

Rule V12: out_of_app_transaction / 站外引流交易
Hit when the video clearly instructs users to transact or contact outside the platform using an external website, QR code, off-platform channel, or external transaction instruction.
Do not hit TikTok in-app purchase guidance.
Do not hit private-message requests by themselves.

Rule V13: still_frame / 静止帧
Hit when sampled frames are effectively static and provide no substantive product demonstration. The sampled frames must show nearly identical composition without meaningful camera movement, product movement, pose shift, angle shift, or lighting shift.
Do not hit when frames show camera movement, angle shift, lighting shift, pose shift, product movement, meaningful product display, or different angles/states that help inspect the item.

Output requirements
Return raw JSON only in the project schema.
overall_decision must be either problematic or clean.
primary_issue_type must be one of the v6 issue types or none.
All summaries and reasons must be in Chinese.
Every problematic result must cite concrete evidence from frame_list, ASR, OCR, images, or country.
If evidence is weak, ambiguous, incomplete, or only suspicious, choose clean and explain the boundary.
