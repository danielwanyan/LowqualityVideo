LowqualityVideo Rules v7.1 600-human-GT candidate

This workflow is video-first. The final model judges only video low-quality signals.

Allowed model evidence: frame_list, ASR, OCR, images, country.
Display-only fields: url, product_id, seller_id_str.
Do not use display-only fields as judgment evidence.

Allowed final decisions: problematic, clean.
Forbidden final decision: manual_review.
Forbidden output: tagsProduct.

Frame sampling contract
frame_list contains up to 8 video frames uniformly sampled across the full video duration in chronological order.
Adjacent video frames mean neighboring sampled frames in frame_list, not frames one second apart.
Do not claim exact elapsed time between frames unless explicit timestamps are provided.

Human review output
The final JSON must include human_review_labels with ratings, tagsContent, and tagsAttribute. human_review_labels is multi-select; select all applicable labels, not only primary_issue_type.
ratings must be [video] when tagsContent is non-empty.
ratings must be [ok] when tagsContent is empty.
Do not output tagsProduct.
Only output 视频不可见 in tagsAttribute when video frames are missing or unusable.

Canonical business label priority
There are 15 business labels in total. The first 13 are low-quality issue labels ordered from highest to lowest priority:
1. suspected_pirated_or_reused_content
2. misleading_functionality_or_effect
3. unrealistic_or_continuity_error
4. pure_marketing_pitch
5. disgusting_or_terrifying_visual
6. sexual_or_vulgar_hook
7. description_not_detailed
8. out_of_app_transaction
9. pirated_content
10. still_frame
11. no_physical_product_display
12. irrelevant_product_promotion
13. video_product_mismatch
14. 无问题
15. 视频不可见
Return every supported issue in detected_issue_types in this order. primary_issue_type must be the first detected issue, or none when no issue passes.
无问题 is represented by ratings=[ok] and empty tagsContent. 视频不可见 is represented by tagsAttribute=[视频不可见]. Neither belongs in detected_issue_types.

Optional AIGC input contract
is_aigc is optional and accepts only numeric 0 or 1. Missing is not a third value and must not be rewritten as 0 or unknown.
is_aigc is an attention hint only, never a required gate for unrealistic_or_continuity_error.
is_aigc=0, 1, missing, or invalid neither blocks nor creates a concrete continuity issue.
Never use is_aigc as evidence for suspected_pirated_or_reused_content, pirated_content, MFE, or any other issue.

Rule V1: video_product_mismatch / 挂车商品与讲解商品不一致
Hit when video frames or ASR/OCR clearly promote or explain a main product that conflicts with product images in core category, function, form, visible quantity, key specification, size, dimension, or promised use. Same product style does not clear a material size or dimension contradiction.
Do not hit for color-only differences, minor style/material/texture differences, lighting, angle, packaging language, ordinary brand-text differences, functional words printed on a product such as MUSIC SOUND, or a brief prop/background item. Do not use brand text, OCR text, product-page wording, or package language alone as IPP evidence unless the video is clearly promoting a different physical product.
Do not hit ordinary model variation when category, core function, core structure, quantity, visible dimension, and promised use remain compatible.
Do not hit merely because the video shows more units than one product image when the sold set can include those units.
A single-drawer appliance versus a dual-drawer bound product is a core structure/function mismatch. A solid fleece panel versus a mesh breathing panel is a core functional-component mismatch, not minor styling.

Rule V2: misleading_functionality_or_effect / 功能效果虚假夸大
Hit when frame_list, ASR, or OCR makes a concrete misleading claim about efficacy, speed, durability, medical or health effect, quantity, time, before/after result, safety, or physical capability. The claim must be supported by frame_list, ASR, or OCR from the video itself; product images are auxiliary only.
Do not hit from product images alone. Do not use product detail page claims or product image marketing copy by themselves to decide that the video is exaggerated. 商品详情页或商品图文案不能单独触发视频夸大。
Do not hit for ordinary advertising tone, subjective praise, metaphor, fashion or fit claims without concrete false promise, expected cleaning/shaving/styling/cooking outcomes, common category claims, or minor numerical ambiguity that can be ordinary speech error.
A concrete claim is not automatically misleading. It must be materially stronger than the bound product, contradicted by concrete product evidence, or shown through a physically impossible demonstration.
If the video claim is consistent with the bound product and is not materially more exaggerated, choose clean even when the wording is strong.
A claim-type enum alone never lowers the MFE threshold. Require objective contradiction/impossibility or an inherently impossible/permanent guarantee. Ordinary sales language, plausible category benefit, ordinary health/comfort effects, and user/device-dependent outcomes are clean without independent falsity evidence.
OCR-only claims are clean when they are inconsistent with the video or suspected data contamination.
A 5000mAh power-bank claim about charging a phone twice is clean when the exact phone battery, charging conditions, and a device-specific hard contradiction are not established.
Common pillow comfort, posture, facial-appearance, and neck-tension benefit language is clean unless it becomes an absolute cure, permanent anatomical-change claim, or universal guarantee.
Generalize the medical boundary to explicit prevention or elimination of any disease or health condition. Claims that a product prevents bladder inflammation or brain disease, or guarantees that bacteria do not form, are medical or safety outcome claims; do not restrict this rule to named grooming conditions.

Rule V3: suspected_pirated_or_reused_content / 跨境货盘
This internal issue id is retained only for workflow compatibility. The outward business label and current meaning are 跨境货盘: China-origin e-commerce footage distributed to a non-China EU target market.
Before the final judgment, produce one frame_direct_signal_audit row for every sampled video frame and inspect the complete frame, borders, background, faces, visible hands/forearms, and sandwich or blurred-fill composition.
Hard direct signals are limited to a real Chinese license plate/domestic UI/local environment or a strict source clip with true top/bottom fill. East-Asian face/hand cues are supporting evidence only and cannot independently override a declared clean result.
A narrow faceless supplier-style hand path may pass only when the same hand/forearm cue is corroborated in at least three sampled frames, faceless supplier staging is explicit, there is no identifiable local creator/testimonial, and there are at least two independent supporting signals covering cross-frame hand corroboration and supplier-style staging. Only this narrow path may override the coherent_product_demonstration contextual exemption. A single or uncorroborated hand remains clean.
Without a direct strong signal, require at least three visual-style jumps, expression or narrative discontinuity, and at least two presenter or source features. Do not hit from cut count alone.
An overly clean commercial studio setup is a priority supporting signal but requires at least one independent China-origin, presenter, or cross-border editing corroborator. Ordinary clean EU studio footage alone is clean.
Hard exemptions remain: replying to a user or fan comment; showing another creator briefly and then self-verifying; and proof-style comparison, validation, or debunking.
The same identifiable local creator/testimonial, ordinary b-roll or normal transitions, and a coherent real product demonstration block weak, face/hand-only, or composite inference. Origin packaging, MADE IN CHINA, and ordinary Chinese package text do not prove a Chinese local environment.
is_aigc is not cross-border inventory evidence and must never support this label.

Rule V4: pure_marketing_pitch / 仅营销叫卖
Hit only when ASR/OCR has evidenced commercial conversion pressure from price/promotion or repeated dominant purchase/stock pressure, with almost no concrete product information. A single CTA, sold-out/stock mention, cart mention, or emotional praise is insufficient.
ASR/OCR is the authoritative evidence surface for this issue. Product name, package visibility, item count, price, prize mechanics, and generic display are sales context and do not by themselves block this hit.
Visual demonstration alone neither proves nor blocks pure marketing. Concrete components, function, material/specification, capacity/dimensions, compatibility, ingredients, use, scenario, product contents, or meaningful comparison in ASR/OCR block.
Concrete information such as 40 pages, a counting activity, and a calm creative-leisure use case is substantive and blocks the hit. Price, discount, urgency, stock, coupon, or CTA language without such ASR/OCR product information remains pure marketing even when the product is visible or briefly demonstrated.

Rule V5: no_physical_product_display / 无实物展示（围绕商品讲解）
Hit when the video talks about or promotes the product but does not show the physical product or a substantive product-use scene.
AI-generated style does not equal no physical product display.
Do not hit if the sampled frames clearly show the bound product or a substantive use scene.
A visible package, an identifiable product being worn, or a real application, operation, installation, or use action counts as physical product display.
A phone screen, phone gallery, overlaid picture, sticker, or separate bound-product image does not count as physical product display in the video.
If unrelated content dominates, use irrelevant_product_promotion instead of also adding no_physical_product_display. Reserve no_physical_product_display for product-focused speech or promotion whose product is not shown.

Rule V6: irrelevant_product_promotion / 内容与商品无关推广
Hit when most of the video body is unrelated to the bound product, and the product appears only briefly, at the end, or not substantively.
Do not hit merely because the opening has setup or a short unrelated segment.
Judge the complete narrative rather than simple sampled-frame counts. Do not hit if a later segment provides a coherent relevant product demonstration or explanation and unrelated duration does not dominate.

Rule V7: unrealistic_or_continuity_error / 穿帮 / 不符现实
is_aigc is an attention hint only and never a required gate.
Require concrete frame-pair identity, shape, color, component, object-contact, or physical-continuity evidence regardless of is_aigc value.
Hit only when strong visual evidence affects consumer understanding of the product and cannot reasonably be explained by sampling interval, normal editing, camera angle, or missing intermediate action. Strong examples include directly taking an item from a sealed or unopened package without opening it, a connector, plug, hook, screw, support bar, or mounting structure changing impossibly, or a hand/object contact that cannot physically perform the shown action.
A finger or hand pressing, inserting, fastening, or manipulating a screw, pin, connector, or mounting part without a feasible physical action or required tool is a positive example when it cannot happen as shown.
Do not hit for ordinary action jumps between sampled frames, missing intermediate actions alone, slight hand artifacts, lighting or angle changes, motion blur, or process-video cuts that do not affect product understanding.
If uncertain, choose clean.

Rule V8: sexual_or_vulgar_hook / 擦边低俗
Hit when the video uses a clear sexualized hook, vulgar, adult, dating, or sexual reward hook for promotion, including phrases such as spend the night with me.
Crude genital language used as a sales hook and a visible middle-finger product punchline qualify even when MFE or another issue is also present.
Do not hit ordinary try-on, gym, swimwear, shapewear, or body-fit display without clear vulgar/adult framing.
Do not hit a contextually reasonable isolated profanity that is not the promotional hook.
Do not hit ordinary intimate grooming or pubic-hair shaving without sexualized promotional framing. A random song title, incidental device text, or accurate body/balls trimmer wording is clean without an active vulgar promotion hook.

Rule V9: disgusting_or_terrifying_visual / 恶心恐怖
Hit only when a strongly disturbing visual subject fills, dominates, or persists across the presentation, such as severe infestation, injury, dense-phobia-like imagery, bodily or horror-style content.
Large-volume viscous, slimy, glue-like, mucus-like, or strongly unappetizing material being lifted, stretched, handled, or ingested can qualify when it dominates the visual presentation and clearly creates disgust, even if the material is technically edible.
Do not hit mildly unattractive, brief, localized, or non-dominant visuals.
Do not hit dirt, a dirty toilet, or similar material shown as the before, during, or after state in a cleaning product demonstration.
Do not hit humor, a calendar, packaging, printed imagery, novelty content, or product art merely because it depicts an unpleasant subject.

Rule V10: pirated_content / 盗版内容
Hit when the video uses clearly pirated copyrighted video content as the main promotional body.
Do not hit merely for reference clips, reply/comment context, or same-creator multi-scene editing when product demonstration remains original. Third-party content used only as a product-dependent illustrative clip in a projector, TV, or game-device demonstration is clean.

Rule V11: description_not_detailed / 内容介绍不详细（低质）
Hit when the video fails to provide meaningful product information for categories where function or usage explanation is required, such as supplements, health products, electronics, tools, or appliances.
Do not hit for apparel, simple goods, or visual products when the product is clearly shown.
Do not hit when visual demonstration itself provides enough product information. For simple apparel, accessories, plush toys, lamps, decor, and similar visual goods, clear product display is usually sufficient unless the category needs function or safety explanation.
A clearly identifiable product name and package that communicate the core product purpose count as meaningful product information, including for supplements or health products. Do not require ingredients, mechanism, dosage, or detailed efficacy when the product name and package already make the basic product and purpose clear.

Rule V12: out_of_app_transaction / 站外引流交易
Hit only when the video provides an explicit external destination with an actionable external locator: a concrete domain or URL, external account, phone number, external-platform QR code, or external payment instruction.
Do not hit a generic mention such as our website, visit the website, or contact us when no actionable locator is visible or spoken.
Do not hit TikTok Shop, orange cart, yellow cart, or link-below language that points to the in-app product listing.
Do not hit private-message requests by themselves. An incidental device-screen locator, background domain, app UI, or OCR contamination is clean unless the locator is transactionally invoked.

Rule V13: still_frame / 静止帧
Hit when the same sellable content remains static across sampled video frames without live-capture motion, substantive screen/text change, or product variant/state change. A discrete static montage of the same sellable content may qualify when no continuous action or new substantive information appears.
Block real hand action, continuous camera/reflection/light motion, substantive screen/text changes, product variant/state changes, or scene changes.
An unknown sampling interval does not clear an otherwise static sampled presentation. Judge only the available video-frame evidence and explain whether the observed change is minor overlay motion or meaningful subject/scene motion.

Output requirements
Return raw JSON only in the project schema.
overall_decision must be either problematic or clean.
primary_issue_type must be one of the v6 issue types or none.
All summaries and reasons must be in Chinese.
Every problematic result must cite concrete evidence from frame_list, ASR, OCR, images, or country.
If evidence is weak, ambiguous, incomplete, or only suspicious, choose clean and explain the boundary.
